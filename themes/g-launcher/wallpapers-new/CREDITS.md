# Replacement wallpapers: sources and licences

All files are 1080 x 2340 WebP, cropped to portrait from the originals. Cropping and
re-encoding are the only changes. Sourced 15 November 2026.

Check each licence yourself before publishing a paid pack. The attribution lines
below belong in the app's Licences screen.

Source: https://github.com/elementary/wallpapers (per-image terms in `debian/copyright`
on the `deb-packaging` branch).

licence. Both restrict selling unaltered copies, which is a risk inside a paid pack.
